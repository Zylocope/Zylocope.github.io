Nexus Sight

A predictive draft-analysis engine for League of Legends esports. Trained on 3,000+ professional matches across LCK, LPL, LEC, LCS and Worlds 2022–2024.

  • XGBoost classifier at 71% validation accuracy — well above coin-flip on a genuinely noisy problem
  • Engineered features rather than raw win rates: a counter score from an 8,900-matchup matrix (56% importance), a custom Elo adjusted for regional strength (35%), composition archetypes (9%)
  • React draft interface returning inference in under 100ms as champions are picked
  • Flags off-role picks and structural gaps — no frontline, all-magic damage
